Our Story

From Peshawar
to Khan Market

Faqir Chand and Sons is a legendary, family-run bookstore in New Delhi's Khan Market, originally established in Peshawar in 1931 before relocating to Delhi in 1951 following the Partition. It is celebrated for its old-world charm, maze-like stacks of books, and deep roots in the capital's literary community.

From timeless Indian history and philosophy to contemporary global fiction, robust Urdu poetry, and classic Hindi literature, our curated collection reflects the diverse minds of the readers we serve.
